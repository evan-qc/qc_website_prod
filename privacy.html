Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy.

Short, because this site does very little. Last updated {{ 'now' | date: "%B %Y" }}.

The short version

This website does not track you. There is no analytics package, no advertising pixel, no cookie banner, and no cookies set by us. The only information we receive is what you deliberately send us through the contact form or by email.

The contact form

Our contact form is hosted by Airtable and embedded on our Let's Talk page. When you submit it, what you type goes to Airtable and into our records: your name, your email address, your organization, and whatever you tell us about your project.

We use that to reply to you and to run the engagement if we end up working together. We do not sell it, we do not rent it, and we do not add you to a marketing list you did not ask for. Because the form is an embedded Airtable page, Airtable's own privacy policy also applies to the submission.

Email

If you email us at {{ site.email }}, we keep the correspondence for as long as we need it to do business with you, the same way any firm keeps its email.

Hosting and fonts

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like nearly every web host, GitHub records standard server request logs, which include IP addresses. We do not have access to those logs and we do not analyze them. See GitHub's privacy statement.

A small number of pages load typefaces from Google Fonts, which means your browser makes a request to Google to fetch them. See Google's privacy policy.

Client data

This policy covers this website only. Data we handle inside a client engagement is governed by that engagement's agreement, not by this page.

Questions, or asking us to delete something

Email {{ site.email }} and ask. If you want us to delete a form submission or your correspondence, say so and we will, unless we are required to keep it for an active contract.
