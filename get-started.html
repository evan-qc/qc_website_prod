Frequently Asked Questions

Do you have an average project cost?

Our pricing is primarily based on the value we deliver to your organization, rather than the number of hours invested in the project. Project costs typically range from $10K to $50K+, depending on the scope and impact.

How do municipalities and agencies contract with you?

As a professional services provider. In New York, professional services are generally exempt from competitive bidding requirements, which means most of our municipal engagements are contracted directly rather than through a formal bid.

That said, procurement is set locally, and we work within whatever your policy requires. If your county or municipality has a General Municipal Law §104-b procurement policy that calls for written or verbal quotes at your spending threshold, tell us the threshold and we will supply what the policy asks for in the format it asks for.

We have a W-9, a certificate of insurance, and client references available on request, and we can provide them before you start the paperwork rather than after. If your purchasing officer wants to talk through the mechanics before anything is committed, we are happy to have that call.

What type of clients do you work with?

Counties, authorities, agencies, school districts, and local employers. Most of our work is in Lewis, Jefferson, and St. Lawrence counties. We also work across upstate New York, and we take on delivery work for consulting firms outside the region.

Does my physical location matter?

Being close helps, and a lot of our clients are within an hour's drive of Lowville, which means we can be in the room when it matters. But it isn't a requirement. We're open to discussing all project inquiries, regardless of where you're based.

How long does a typical engagement take?

Engagement length varies with scope - from a focused 1–2 week data strategy workshop to multi-month implementation projects. We'll scope this together during your free consultation.

What happens after the build is finished?

We stay on. Systems need an owner, and most of our clients don't have an internal team to hand one to. When we finish a build we remain the team that maintains it, so when something breaks or changes there's a name attached. More on how we work →
